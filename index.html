How a Partnership Works

What stays yours. What gets stronger.

Private equity buys to sell. Strategic buyers absorb and rebrand. We do neither. We buy to own, and we leave what works alone.

What stays yours

  • Your company name and brand
  • Your team and your culture
  • Your client relationships
  • Your seat leading the business

What gets stronger

  • Capital to hire and grow
  • Shared security, compliance and legal support
  • Peers across the platform to learn from
  • Help with finance and reporting

What we never do

  • Hold you to a fund timeline
  • Rebrand or absorb your firm
  • Contact your clients or staff without your consent
  • Rush your decision
Why These Sectors

Essential work. Steady demand.

Security and healthcare are services people cannot go without. Much of that work is done by small, founder owned companies that know their clients and patients by name.

Those companies do essential work, often without the capital or the succession plan to keep doing it for decades. That is the gap Cyber Profound exists to close.

Standards

The standards we build to.

Security and healthcare are judged against clear standards. These are the frameworks we use to assess the companies we consider and to guide how platform companies operate.

HIPAA Security Rule

The federal baseline for protecting electronic health information.

HHS 405(d) HICP

Health Industry Cybersecurity Practices published for care organizations of every size.

NIST Cybersecurity Framework

The common language for measuring and improving a security program.

HITRUST

A certifiable framework widely requested by health systems and payers.

SOC 2

Independent assurance over how a service provider protects client data.

Listed as the frameworks that guide our work. This is not a statement of certification.
